# Privacy Policy — Muse

_Last updated: September 22, 2026_

Muse ("the app") is an open-source text-to-speech client. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is processed.

## Summary

The app's developer does **not** collect, store, or share any personal data. All processing that involves network access happens between your device and the [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) API, using **your own** ElevenLabs API key and account.

## Data the app stores (on your device only)

- **ElevenLabs API key** — saved in the app's local preferences so you stay signed in to the API. It is never sent anywhere except as an authentication header to `api.elevenlabs.io`.
- **Your texts and generated audio files** — projects and exported MP3/WAV files are stored on your device (app storage or the location you export to).
- **Crash logs** — if the app crashes, diagnostic logs are written locally and can be viewed in the app's crash-log screen. They are not uploaded to the developer or any third party.

## Data sent to third-party services

When you use speech synthesis, audio isolation, speech-to-text and related features, the app sends the content you provide (text, and/or audio you select) to the ElevenLabs API (`api.elevenlabs.io`) so it can be processed. This happens under **your own ElevenLabs account** and is governed by the [ElevenLabs privacy policy](https://elevenlabs.io/privacy-policy). The developer of Muse has no access to this data and operates no server that receives it.

## Data the developer collects

None. Muse has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no telemetry. The developer does not receive your API key, your texts, your audio, or your crash logs.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the [Muse repository](https://github.com/kkoshin/Muse) and the "last updated" date will change.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at [github.com/kkoshin/Muse/issues](https://github.com/kkoshin/Muse/issues).
